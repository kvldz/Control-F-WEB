Three steps. No typing.

Control F does the sorting, naming, and remembering for you.

  1. Store

    Point your camera at the item and say where it goes. It takes 2 seconds.

    "Control F, I put the spare keys under the bedside table."

  2. Index

    The AI listens to what you said and watches what the camera sees. It saves both as a memory you can search. You never name, tag, or sort anything.

  3. Search

    Days or weeks later, ask where something is. Control F answers out loud and plays the clip of you putting it there.

    "Where are the scissors?"
